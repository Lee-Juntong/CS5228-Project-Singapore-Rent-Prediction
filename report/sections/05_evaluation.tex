The simple models get closer than we expected. With the index target, the group median lookup is 37~SGD behind tuned LightGBM and Ridge is 22~SGD behind, against 305 and 126~SGD on raw rent. Linear models and KNN use restricted inputs (34 and 4 columns), so these gaps do not isolate the algorithm alone. Boosting adds little over a forest on these data. On raw rent tuned boosting beats the forest by 9 to 10~SGD, and with the index target the untuned forest beats tuned boosting by 1 to 5~SGD. Tuning gains 4 to 7~SGD on raw rent. The top three single models lie within 1.5~SGD of each other, and tuned XGBoost and LightGBM are 0.05~SGD apart on the public leaderboard, so we treat them as a tie.

We retrained tuned LightGBM on the index target without the flagged rows in the fit period, with features and hyperparameters fixed. Dropping the 44 fit period rows (49 in all) with a low rent for their area changes the holdout RMSE by $-0.1$~SGD and the public score by $-0.4$~SGD. Dropping the 10 fit period rows (12 in all) flagged by both of our rules adds 0.4~SGD. Dropping the 276 fit period rows (331 in all) that are extreme within their flat type or town adds 2.2~SGD on the holdout and 4.2~SGD on the public leaderboard, probably because that rule also removes real premium rents. We keep all rows.

\subsection{Final Model}

Table~\ref{tab:blend} compares the blends on the holdout, the 16 month window and the public leaderboard. The two model blend is tuned LightGBM plus the regularised LightGBM, and the index and raw mix fits these two models on both targets. The repeat-rent features and the blend gain on all three: the five model blend beats tuned LightGBM by 14.8~SGD on the holdout, 18.3~SGD on the 16 month window and 19.4~SGD on the public score. The regularised LightGBM alone gains 9.9 and 13.2~SGD on the first two. On the 16 month window the index models over-predict by about 120~SGD, because the slope fitted in late 2023 was too steep. The mix of index and raw targets cuts this to 87~SGD and has the best 16 month score, but its public score is 4.6~SGD worse.
