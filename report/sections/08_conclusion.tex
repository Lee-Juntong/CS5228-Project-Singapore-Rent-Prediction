\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We forecast HDB rents up to 16 months past the training data. The five model blend is our final Kaggle submission, with an RMSE of 431.0~SGD and a MAPE of 11.75\% on the holdout, and 415.0~SGD on the public leaderboard. Complex models beat simple ones by little once the trend is handled: Ridge is 22~SGD behind tuned LightGBM, and an untuned forest matches tuned boosting. The repeat-rent features and the blend gained 14.8~SGD on the holdout, more than tuning (4 to 7~SGD) or the choice of boosting library. Flat type, the two year rent lags and location drive the predictions. Repeated holdout profiles still have a pooled within-group standard deviation of about 450~SGD.
